Put your images in this folder using these exact file names:

  logo.png                          - Clinic logo (header, footer, favicon)
  hero-heal-from-the-root.jpeg       - Hero poster "Heal from the Root"
  ayurvedic-cycle.jpeg               - About section "Ayurvedic Cycle" poster
  shirodhara.jpeg                    - Panchkarma section (large image)
  netra-tarpan.jpeg                  - Panchkarma section (small image, left)
  saundarya-chikitsa.jpeg            - Panchkarma section (small image, right)
  product-suvarnaprashan.jpeg        - Product 1
  product-arogyaprash.jpeg           - Product 2
  product-suwarnaprashan-drops.jpeg  - Product 3
  gallery-panchkarma-chikitsa.jpeg   - Gallery 1
  gallery-saundarya-chikitsa.jpeg    - Gallery 2
  gallery-shirodhara.jpeg            - Gallery 3
  gallery-netra-tarpan.jpeg          - Gallery 4

Until an image is added, a placeholder box showing its alt text is displayed.
Want a different file name or format (.png/.webp)? Just change the src="" in index.html
(or the PRODUCTS list at the top of js/main.js for product images).
